Introduction

Established around 1930, Uttor Guaton Jame Mosque has long served as the spiritual heart of the local Muslim community. Due to its age, the mosque is now in a severely deteriorated condition. A renovation was carried out approximately 10 years ago, but the structure has since deteriorated significantly and requires urgent attention again.

Due to a lack of sufficient local resources, completing the renovation has become increasingly difficult. The overall infrastructure has weakened to a point where partial repairs are no longer a viable solution — complete reconstruction is the only safe and sustainable path forward.

Reconstruction Plan

Demolition & Groundwork

Main Structure Construction

  • Reinforced RCC foundation

Interior Works
